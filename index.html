Privacy Policy

YE Social Proof & Sales Popup
Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how YE Social Proof & Sales Popup collects, uses, stores, and deletes information when a merchant installs and uses the app on a Shopify store.

The app is designed to display authentic recent-purchase notifications, social proof, promotional popups, offers, shipping messages, product launches, seasonal campaigns, and other storefront announcements.

2. Information the App Accesses

The app may access limited order and product information from the merchant's Shopify store in order to provide its features.

For recent-purchase notifications, the app may process and store only the following information:

  • Customer first name
  • Customer city
  • Product title
  • Product image
  • Order number
  • Order date

3. Information the App Does Not Store

The app does not store the following customer information:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Street address
  • Payment information
  • Credit card information

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the app's functionality.

This includes:

  • Displaying authentic recent-purchase notifications
  • Showing product information inside storefront popups
  • Providing merchant-configured promotional campaigns
  • Managing app settings and merchant preferences
  • Operating and maintaining the app

Sales proof notifications are generated from real Shopify order data. The app does not fabricate purchases or create fake order timestamps.

5. Promotional Campaigns

Promotional campaign messages are created directly by the merchant. These messages may include offers, shipping promotions, product launches, seasonal campaigns, announcements, or other promotional content.

Promotional campaign messages are separate from customer order data.

6. Data Retention

Customer-related recent-purchase notification data is retained for a maximum of 30 days.

The app automatically runs a scheduled daily cleanup process to delete customer-related sales notification data that is older than the retention period.

7. Data Deletion and Shopify Privacy Requests

The app supports Shopify's required privacy and compliance workflows.

When Shopify sends an applicable customer or shop deletion request, the app processes the request and deletes the relevant data in accordance with Shopify's requirements.

8. Data Sharing

The app does not sell customer or merchant data.

The app does not use customer data for behavioral advertising.

Data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers that are necessary to operate the app, such as hosting and database providers. These providers are used only to provide the technical infrastructure required for the app to function.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by the app.

Access to Shopify data is limited to the permissions required for the app's functionality, and sensitive credentials are not exposed to the storefront or browser.

10. Merchant Responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for ensuring that the content they create and display through promotional campaigns complies with applicable laws, regulations, and Shopify policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be available at this page.
